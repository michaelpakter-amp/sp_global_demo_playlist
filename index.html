Amplitude Platform Overview: AI-Powered Analytics, Replay and Product Insights

This recording is the first full walkthrough of the Amplitude platform, built around S&P’s priorities of understanding user workflows, account-level usage, and what to build next. Using a demo environment, it covers how data gets into Amplitude, how users and accounts are modeled, and how built-in AI helps teams get from a question to evidence to action.

Highlights

  • Flexible data collection. Events come in through the SDK, which can be served through a proxy so it appears first-party. They can also come from a CDP or a data warehouse.
  • Users, accounts and platforms. User profiles with identity stitching from anonymous to logged-in. Organizations, projects and portfolio views, and account-level tracking across multiple platforms.
  • Ask questions in plain language. The Global Agent answers questions with key findings, funnel breakdowns, suggested experiments and supporting session replays.
  • From chart to replay. Jump from a funnel drop-off to the matching session replays, with AI summaries and sentiment.
  • Agents that watch for you. Dashboard and Session Replay agents monitor for anomalies and send results by email, Slack or Teams. Cohorts such as light, medium and power users can be defined with the agent’s help.
  • Wave for deciding what to build next. Wave combines analytics, replays and customer feedback into prioritized opportunities with evidence, proposed solutions and acceptance criteria. It can hand work to coding agents with human approval.
